- During the hike we came across various Thistels which were so big, I found that they looked like cacti more than a thistle. At home, we view them as weeds, but looking this impressive I wouldn't mind having one at home.
- We walked near a water well, where we had to walk just next to the cliff. 
    - There were gates and no cell service
    - I thought we were lost
- After this, we were quickly declining into a forest area where we couldn't see the route at all
- Ending very scary
- The GPX path just ended
    - Only displayed a straight line from the top of the mountain to the hotel
- Very steep descent with loose rocks and sand
- During the descent we could see our hotel and it looked so close
- Every turn looked as if we were going away from the hotel and we were tired and wanted to be able to take off our shoes
- Small kiosk where we had a cool drink
- Hotel had an actual hot tub
    - We went in even though it was over 26&deg;C outside
