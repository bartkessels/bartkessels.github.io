This hike started off by a bus driver picking us up to bring us to a town a couple of kilometers away from the hotel. Since our bus driver didn't speak English very well, he waved us to follow him once we arrived at Serato. From there he just pointed to the edge of town and that was our cue that our hike would start there.

The start brought us to the bottom of another climb, this was an uphill climb that we weren't yet expecting. The climb was pretty steep and next to the road there were a lot of olive groves where we could hear tractors working. Eventually we saw a tractor driving all tilted since the groves where just as steep as the road we were walking on.

- We went and had dinner in the mountains, surrounded by various herbs we grow at home as well.
    - This hike I did bring my chair
- Ardales was a very nice place
- Hotel with an extremely cold swimming pool and "hot" tub
- Best fries (Patatas Gratinadas Bravas a La Equus)
    - Small bar called Pub EQUUS
