This isn't really a laid out trail, but it's a route we walked in Andalusia, Spain.

A couple of months ago we received mail from the previous owners of our house from [Ecktiv](https://ecktiv.nl/). They are a company that plans out routes and books all the accommodations for various types of active holidays. After we've gotten that letter we were looking around on their website and we found a route in Spain that looked really good. That's when we decided, let's do it! Next thing we know, we're booking the trip through [Ecktiv](https://ecktiv.nl/) and looking at airplane tickets to Malaga.

  The route we'll be hiking goes from Ronda to Antequera, which is about 100 kilometers. Each day we'll be walking about 15 kilometers going up and down various mountains. Because we're from the Netherlands we never really walked through a mountain area, so this is going to be a new experience for us.

  One of the highlights that we already saw on the website is the famous Caminito del Rey, which is a trail that's built to the wall of a cliff. As you can see in the image, it's really next to the cliff.
